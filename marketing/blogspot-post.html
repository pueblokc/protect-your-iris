The Hard-Won Discoveries

Here's the thing about Blue Iris 6 + UniFi Protect integration: the official documentation for both products essentially says "use RTSP" and leaves you to figure out the rest. There's a lot of rest.

Some of the things we discovered the hard way:

ip_subpath is the actual sub-stream field in the registry. Not ip_path2. If you search for "Blue Iris sub stream path" you'll find exactly zero documentation about this.

MEDIUM quality RTSP tokens fail on ~40% of cameras. Always use HIGH for main streams and LOW for sub streams. Never MEDIUM.

ip_port must be set to 80, not 7447. Yes, RTSP runs on 7447. But Blue Iris wants 80 here. Don't ask why. Just accept it.

RTSP tokens are permanent. They don't expire. That's not documented anywhere. We tested it.

BI6 overwrites registry keys while running. If you edit the registry while Blue Iris is running, your changes will be wiped the moment you click anything in the UI. Stop the service first. Every time.
